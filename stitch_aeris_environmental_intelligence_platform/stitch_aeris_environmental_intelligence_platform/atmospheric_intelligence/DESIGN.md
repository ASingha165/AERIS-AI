## Brand & Style
The design system positions itself at the intersection of orbital telemetry, real-time planetary sensing, and generative prediction. Built for municipal operators, environmental scientists, and enterprise climate strategists, the interface balances executive clarity with dense analytical power.

The aesthetic philosophy is **Atmospheric Command**:
- Deep stratospheric dark surfaces with progressive luminous elevations.
- Precise, synthetic accents (mint emerald and cold troposphere cyan) signaling vitality, sensor health, and algorithmic certainty.
- Refined glass surfaces simulating multi-layered radar overlays, atmospheric particle strata, and clean analytical HUD panels.
- Micro-interactions characterized by measured kinetic responsiveness, subtle edge illuminations, and zero-latency data telemetry feedback.

## Colors
The system employs an emissive color hierarchy calibrated specifically for prolonged operational observation in low-light command environments while exceeding WCAG 2.1 AA legibility.

### Surface Architecture
- **Canvas Base (`#080F1C`):** Deep abyssal navy serving as the foundational background viewport.
- **Surface Layer 1 (`#101C2B`):** Secondary container surface for navigation rails, toolbars, and contextual sidebars.
- **Surface Layer 2 (`#142235`):** Standard analytical card, widget, and modal base.
- **Surface Border (`#1F334E`):** Hairline boundary tone, separating overlapping data streams without hard visual barriers.

### Luminescence & Telemetry Accents
- **Primary Signal (`#37E6B5`):** High-energy mint emerald reserved for primary actionable items, model convergence, and positive atmospheric baselines.
- **Secondary Stream (`#53C7F5`):** Tropospheric sky cyan for live air currents, predictive vector envelopes, and informational indices.

### Text & Metrics
- **Primary Readout (`#F5FAFF`):** Polar white with high contrast against container darks.
- **Secondary Readout (`#A5B7C8`):** Subdued steel mist for axis markers, metadata, and structural micro-copy.

### Environmental Semantic States
- **Nominal / Cleared (`#43D9A3`):** Clean air indices (AQI 0–50), active hardware nodes, verified predictions.
- **Elevated / Caution (`#F6C453`):** Particulate spike thresholds (AQI 51–100), sensor drift, inversion warning.
- **Severe / Critical (`#F16B77`):** Hazardous particulate saturation (AQI 150+), pipeline failures, active urban pollution incidents.

## Typography
Typography creates a stark separation between quantitative telemetry and discursive qualitative intelligence:

- **Space Grotesk** serves as the mathematical and navigational anchor. It governs atmospheric metrics (PM2.5, PM10, NO2, O3 concentrations), index scores, card headers, and command-level readouts. Its geometric construction provides technical precision without utilitarian brutality.
- **Geist** handles the structural UI infrastructure: tabular sensor logs, AI generation summaries, diagnostic tooltips, and data labels. Its neutral geometry ensures frictionless scanning across multi-panel layouts.
- **Telemetry Readouts (`label-mono`, `label-caps`):** Used strictly for raw hardware status flags, coordinate coordinates, UTC timestamps, and delta indicators. Always rendered in uppercase with positive letter tracking.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout operates as a fluid dashboard architecture organized into responsive situational command zones:

### Screen Adaptation
- **Desktop (1440px+):** 12-column dynamic layout with `gutter-lg` (24px) and `margin-lg` (32px). Persistent collapsed or expanded telemetry sidebar (300px), centered situational map/vector canvas, and contextual generative-insight drawer (380px).
- **Tablet / Laptop (768px - 1439px):** 8-column layout with `gutter` (16px) and `margin-md` (24px). Analytical sidebars collapse into overlay slide-overs or stacked tabbed modules below primary geospatial viewports.
- **Mobile (Under 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with single-column metric stacks, persistent bottom control sheets for model toggles, and swipeable time-series ribbons.

### Density Rules
Use ultra-compact component gaps (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) inside high-density sensor readout matrices and time-step scrubbers. Reserve larger intervals (`space-lg` and `space-xl`) for grouping distinct diagnostic domains and separating AI-assisted predictive timelines from current telemetry.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is produced through atmospheric stratifications, subtle glassmorphism, and edge-lit luminescence rather than heavy dropped drop-shadows.

### Layer Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Spatial Plane):** Hex `#080F1C` backdrop, typically hosting interactive geospatial maps, pollution plume visualizers, or heat-density contours.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Canvas):** Hex `#101C2B` with 85% opacity and an 8px backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`), grounding tool ribbons and secondary panels.
- **Tier 2 (Analytical Modules & Cards):** Hex `#142235` at 92% opacity, bounded by a 1px solid stroke in `#1F334E`. Shadow: `0 8px 32px 0 rgba(4, 8, 16, 0.45)`.
- **Tier 3 (Floating Overlays, Modals, Popovers):** Hex `#16273D` at 95% opacity with a 16px backdrop blur, bordered by `#2E486D` with a subtle primary aura: `box-shadow: 0 16px 48px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(55, 230, 181, 0.15)`.

### Optical Highlights & Glowing Edges
Active cards, alert focal areas, and focused telemetry streams feature a micro-gradient along the top 1px perimeter border transitioning from transparent through `#37E6B5` (35% alpha) back to transparent. This simulates light caught along chamfered dark crystal surfaces.

## Shapes
The visual identity embraces a tailored, refined silhouette balancing soft aerodynamic radii with engineering discipline:

- Standard operational cards, panels, and modal containers utilize curved radii between **14px and 20px** (`rounded-lg` to `rounded-xl`).
- Micro-elements such as badge pills, live streaming indicators, and toggle pills adopt full continuous radii (pill capsules).
- Interactive input fields, dropdown trigger buttons, and tab segment items snap to consistent **8px to 10px** geometry.
- Hairline borders are strictly **1px**; avoid 2px strokes except when designating keyboard focus states or active geospatial selection rings.

## Components

### Buttons & Command Triggers
- **Primary GenAI Action:** Background in solid `#37E6B5`, label in `#080F1C` (`Space Grotesk` medium), border radius 10px. Hover: light emission glow `box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(55, 230, 181, 0.4)`. Active: micro-depress scale transform (0.98).
- **Secondary Ghost / Outline:** Background `rgba(20, 34, 53, 0.6)` with border `1px solid #1F334E`, text `#F5FAFF`. Hover: border shifts to `#53C7F5`, background shifts to `rgba(83, 199, 245, 0.1)`.
- **Destructive Command:** Border `1px solid rgba(241, 107, 119, 0.4)`, text `#F16B77`, background `rgba(241, 107, 119, 0.08)`.

### Analytical Cards & HUD Containers
- Solid `#142235` fill with 1px border `#1F334E`. Padding set to `1.25rem` (`20px`).
- Top-right corner dedicated to real-time status pulses or generative confidence percentage flags.
- Optional inner header divider: hairline horizontal gradient from `#1F334E` fading to transparent.

### Telemetry Badges & AQI Pills
- Compact capsule shapes (`rounded-full`) with padding `2px 8px`.
- Background derived from semantic states at 12% opacity; text matched to semantic hex at 100% opacity; outer border at 25% opacity.
- Includes a leading `6px` circular pulse dot containing an ambient radial blur animation for active real-time data feeds.

### Input Fields & Filter Pickers
- Background `#101C2B` inset within cards, 1px border `#1F334E`.
- Text `#F5FAFF`, placeholder text `#A5B7C8` at 60% opacity.
- Focus state: border-color `#37E6B5`, zero native outline, and a soft glow `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(55, 230, 181, 0.15)`.

### Tabular Lists & Node Feeds
- Alternating rows stripped of heavy striping; separation managed via `1px solid rgba(31, 51, 78, 0.5)` bottom borders.
- Hover row state triggers a transition to `rgba(83, 199, 245, 0.04)` with a 2px `#53C7F5` indicator mark along the left margin.

### Checkboxes, Radios, & Sensor Selectors
- Unselected: `#101C2B` base with `1px solid #1F334E`.
- Selected: Background `#37E6B5` with `#080F1C` check/dot mark.
- Micro-radii: Checkboxes use `4px` rounded corners; radio items use pure circles.

### Specialized AI & Air Quality Components
- **GenAI Prediction Shimmer:** Synthetic forecast curves and simulated future telemetry points render with an animated linear gradient mask travelling along the vector path.
- **Air Quality Gauge (Ring HUD):** Segmented circular meters utilizing `#37E6B5` to `#53C7F5` gradients with ambient drop-shadow matching the active index band.
- **Hardware Health Pill:** Micro-badge containing node ID, latency counter (`14ms`), and green/amber signal bar glyphs.
